# The Royal Rivalry: series plan (12 episodes)

A Clash of Clans fan comic built from official Supercell Fan Kit art. Every episode ends on a cliffhanger
and a comment prompt.

**Format (current):** full episodes of about 10 minutes, 16:9 (1920x1080), voiced by a local Kokoro cast
(narrator plus a voice per character, narrator-only subtitles), each with a ~1-minute vertical promo Short
voiced with ElevenLabs premade voices.
- Full episodes: `projects/coc/royal-rivalry-full-ep<NN>/` (build.py writes script.json)
- Promos: `projects/coc/royal-rivalry-full-ep<NN>-promo/`
- Page helpers and casts: `projects/coc/rivalry.py`; page engine: `motion/coc/comicpage.js`
- The earlier vertical Shorts (`royal-rivalry-ep1..3`) were the pilot of the same story.

**Main cast:** Barbarian King and Archer Queen (rivals), Grand Warden (mentor), the Goblin from Ep 1
(comic relief, later a guide), Barbarians and Archers (the two factions), Healer.
**Villain:** the Electro Titan, a storm sorceress who wants the kingdom's Elixir to power her storm.
**Her forces:** Electro Dragons, a P.E.K.K.A guard. **Late allies:** Royal Champion, Dragons, Balloons.

## Arc 1: The Storm
1. **Who Is the Greatest?** (full episode + promo done): the rivalry, the Warden's contest, the goblin collision, eyes in the dark.
2. **The Storm Arrives** (full episode scripted): an Electro Dragon attacks, the heroes sabotage each other, the Warden falls.
3. **Picking Up the Pieces:** the village in ruins, the Healer saves the Warden, the heroes blame each other;
   the Warden warns the dragon was *sent*; the Queen finds a crackling storm mark. Cliffhanger: who sent it?
4. **The Storm Sorceress:** the mark leads to a vision of the Electro Titan on her floating citadel; she wants
   the kingdom's Elixir. She sends three dragons as a warning. Cliffhanger: the Elixir collectors are emptied.

## Arc 2: Divided
5. **Two Paths:** the King and Queen each set out alone to hunt the Titan, and both fail badly.
6. **The Goblin Who Knew Too Much:** the goblin from Ep 1 used to sell stolen gold to the Titan; he knows the way.
   The heroes must trust a thief.
7. **The Bridge of Trust:** a broken canyon bridge: the King can't jump it, the Queen can't carry the gear.
   First real teamwork: King throws, Queen anchors with arrows.

## Arc 3: United
8. **Ambush in the Clouds:** dragons ambush the party; the Barbarians and Archers arrive *together*, marching as
   one army for the first time.
9. **The Champion's Return:** the Royal Champion, who left the kingdom long ago, joins the fight, and has her own
   history with the Titan.
10. **Siege of the Storm Citadel:** Balloons and Dragons lead the assault; a P.E.K.K.A guards the gate.
11. **Clash of Storms:** the final battle: the Warden returns with his tome to shield the army; the King and Queen
    combine strength and aim to bring down the Electro Titan.
12. **The Greatest Hero:** the kingdom rebuilt; the Warden crowns *both* heroes; a statue of the two together.
    Final gag: they argue over whose side of the statue is taller. Tease for a season 2.

## Rules
- Official Fan Kit art only, scaled / moved / cropped / faded (no recolouring or redrawing characters);
  effects, bubbles and scenery are drawn on top.
- Each video description carries Supercell's Fan Content Policy disclaimer.
- Clash videos use the Clash sound set (not the softened Wuthering Waves set).
