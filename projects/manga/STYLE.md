# Manga Fever house style (asked for by the channel owner)

## Thumbnails (from FUTARI on)
A comic page, not a single picture with a headline:
- Three panels with thin black gutters on a dark frame: two stacked panels on the left, one tall panel on the right
  (about 50/50 width), each a key moment of the story.
- Speech bubbles: white ovals with a black outline and NO tail/arrow, short lines in bold italic capitals.
  Male speakers' words in blue, female speakers' words in pink/magenta.
- A small white rectangle caption box with red capital text for a label (e.g. "ACTRESS").
- Hana (the host) as a small square portrait in the bottom-left corner of the bottom-left panel.
- Optional: a small chibi face inset with a sweat drop for a comic reaction.

## Video captions
- Dialogue: white oval bubbles, no tail, blue words for male speakers, pink for female ones
  (make_video.py caption style "bubbles": {"male": [...], "female": [...]}).
- Narration: the white rectangle box at the top, black words.
