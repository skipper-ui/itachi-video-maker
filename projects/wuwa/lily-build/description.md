# Best Lily Build: Wuthering Waves (Version 3.8)

**Title:** Best Lily Build in Wuthering Waves: Weapon, Echoes, Stats & Teams #shorts

**Description:**
The best Lily build in Wuthering Waves (Version 3.8), in under two minutes.

- Key stat: Energy Regen, aim for about 260% (it powers her buffs and boosts her crit rate)
- Weapon: Lullaby (signature). Static Mist if you own it, Cadenza is the best 4-star
- Echoes for Unison teams: 5-piece Flower of Tinged Yearning, Formrender main echo
- Echoes for Echo Skill / any team: 5-piece Rejuvenating Glow, Fallacy of No Return main echo
- Main stats (4-3-3-1-1): 4-cost HP%, 3-cost Energy Regen, 1-cost HP%
- Substats: Energy Regen, then HP%
- Teams: Lily + Hsin + Suoming (Unison), Lily + Sigrika + Lucilla or Qiuyuan (Echo Skill)
